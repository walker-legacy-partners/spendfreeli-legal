Privacy Policy — SpendFreeli
Effective Date: June 1, 2026
Last Updated: June 1, 2026

1. Introduction
SpendFreeli ("we," "our," or "us") is a personal and family budgeting application published by Walker Legacy Partners, LLC. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. It complies with applicable privacy laws including the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and Australia's Privacy Act 1988.
By using SpendFreeli, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect
2.1 Account Information

Email address — used for account creation, login, and invite delivery
Full name — used to identify you within your family budget group
Mobile phone number — optionally provided to receive SMS invitations to join a family budget. We do not use your phone number for marketing purposes

2.2 Financial Data

Transaction records — amounts, dates, merchants, categories, subcategories, and notes you enter
Budget data — budget templates, monthly budgets, and spending goals you create
Payment method labels — names you assign to payment methods (e.g. "Chase Visa") — we do not collect actual card numbers, bank account numbers, or any sensitive financial credentials

2.3 User-Generated Content

Receipt photos — images you choose to attach to transactions, stored securely in our cloud storage
Notes and descriptions — text you add to transactions and budgets

2.4 Technical Information

Device information — device type and operating system version, collected automatically to ensure app compatibility
App usage data — basic information about how you interact with the app to help us improve the experience

2.5 Minor User Information
When an administrator invites a minor (person under 18) to a family budget:

The administrator confirms they are the parent or legal guardian of the minor
The minor's account is linked to the guardian's account
All data collected for minor users is the same as adult users (sections 2.1-2.4)
Minor users cannot self-register — they may only be invited by a verified parent or guardian

2.6 What We Do NOT Collect

Credit card numbers or bank account credentials
Social Security numbers or government ID numbers
Precise GPS location
Contacts or address book data
Biometric data
Behavioral tracking or advertising identifiers


3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & International)
For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and other jurisdictions requiring a legal basis for data processing, we process your personal data under the following bases:

Contract performance — processing necessary to provide the SpendFreeli service you have signed up for
Legitimate interests — processing necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as improving the app and preventing fraud, where these interests are not overridden by your rights
Legal obligation — processing necessary to comply with applicable laws
Consent — where we have asked for and received your consent, such as optional SMS communication

You may withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis for processing, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

4. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect solely to:

Provide and operate the SpendFreeli app
Enable you to track your personal and family budget
Allow family members you invite to access your shared budget
Send account invitations via email or SMS when requested
Improve app performance and fix issues
Respond to your support requests
Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, sell your data to third parties, or use it for any purpose beyond operating the app.

5. How We Share Your Information
We only share your data with third-party service providers that are strictly necessary to operate SpendFreeli:
5.1 Supabase (Database & Storage Provider)
Your budget and transaction data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase acts as our database and file storage provider. Supabase infrastructure is located in the United States.

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

5.2 Expo (App Build & Delivery Platform)
SpendFreeli is built using the Expo platform, which facilitates app delivery and updates.

Expo Privacy Policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

5.3 Google Play & Apple App Store (In-App Purchases)
If you purchase a paid plan, payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). Walker Legacy Partners, LLC does not collect, process, or store your payment card information. Your billing data is governed by Google's or Apple's respective privacy policies.

Google Play Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy

5.4 SMS Service Provider
If you choose to send or receive invitations via SMS, your mobile phone number will be transmitted to our SMS delivery provider solely for the purpose of delivering that message. Your number is not retained by the SMS provider beyond message delivery.
5.5 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority in any jurisdiction where we operate.
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any other third parties.

6. International Data Transfers
SpendFreeli is operated from the United States. If you are accessing the app from outside the United States, including from the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Canada, or Australia, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States.
We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data receives an adequate level of protection regardless of where it is processed:

EEA/UK users: Data transfers are made pursuant to Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, or other appropriate transfer mechanisms
All international users: Your data is protected by the security measures described in Section 7 of this policy

By using SpendFreeli, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

7. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on Supabase servers with industry-standard encryption
Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
Receipt photos are stored in a private, access-controlled storage bucket
Access to your family's budget data is restricted to members you explicitly invite
We implement role-based access controls (Admin and Member roles) within the app
We conduct regular reviews of our data handling practices

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and keep your login credentials private.

8. Data Retention
We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

Your personal profile and login credentials will be removed within 7 days
Your transaction and budget data will be deleted within 30 days
Receipt photos will be deleted from storage within 30 days
Audit logs may be retained for up to 12 months for security and legal compliance purposes

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
Note for EEA/UK users: We will honor deletion requests within 30 days as required under GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure).

9. Family Members & Shared Data
SpendFreeli is designed for shared family use. When you invite a family member:

They will have access to your shared budget data
Their transactions will be visible to the account administrator
The administrator can manage member access, roles, and spending limits
Removing a member revokes their access to the shared budget

Please only invite people you trust with your financial information.

10. Children and Minor Users
SpendFreeli is a family budgeting tool that supports supervised use by minors (persons under 18) under parental or guardian oversight.
10.1 Children Under 13 (COPPA Compliance)
SpendFreeli does not allow children under 13 to self-register. Children under 13 may only use SpendFreeli when:

Invited by a verified parent or legal guardian who holds the account
The parent or guardian has explicitly confirmed their parental consent during the invitation process

When inviting a minor user, the account administrator (parent or guardian) must:

Confirm they are the parent or legal guardian of the minor
Acknowledge that they consent to the minor's participation and data collection
Accept responsibility for supervising the minor's use of the app

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13 without proper parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.
10.2 Users Under 18
Minor users (ages 13-17) may use SpendFreeli under parental supervision. Their accounts are linked to the parent or guardian's account, and the guardian administrator retains full visibility and control over their activity.
10.3 Contact for Child Privacy Concerns
If you believe a child's information has been collected without proper consent, please contact us immediately at walkerlegacypartners@gmail.com and we will address the matter within 5 business days.
